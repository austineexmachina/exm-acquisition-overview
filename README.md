# EX MACHINA (EXM)

**Cybersecurity architecture concept · Strategic evaluation · Potential IP acquisition**

EX MACHINA (EXM) is an early-stage defensive cybersecurity initiative exploring an integrated architecture for **authorised threat investigation, contextual attack-path analysis, policy-governed response, and auditable recovery**.

This public repository is a **non-confidential acquisition overview** for cybersecurity companies, technology founders, product-strategy teams, and corporate-development professionals in the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, Canada, Australia, Singapore, and elsewhere.

## Proposed direction

- **Investigate:** correlate authorised security signals to identify suspicious behaviour and possible attack paths.
- **Decide:** apply human oversight, scoped permissions, and explicit approval gates to consequential defensive actions.
- **Respond:** explore containment and recovery workflows with rollback and auditable records.
- **Evaluate:** assess engineering feasibility, security controls, interoperability, and commercial relevance through due diligence.

These are **design objectives**, not claims of deployed capabilities or measured performance.
